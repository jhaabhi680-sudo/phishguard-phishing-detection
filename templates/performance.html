Cybersecurity Optimization Criteria

In phishing prevention, a False Negative (allowing an unsuspecting user onto a credential phishing site) is disastrous, whereas a False Positive is a manageable warning. Therefore, our automated model selector prioritizes F1-Score (40%) and Recall (20%) over raw baseline accuracy.
